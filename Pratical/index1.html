CASE STUDY 01

Bus Booking App

A simple and intuitive bus booking experience designed to make searching, comparing and booking buses easier.

↗
ROLE UI/UX Designer
TOOLS Figma · FigJam
DURATION 2 Weeks
TYPE Mobile App
Easy Search
Quick Booking
01

Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a smoother bus booking experience. The goal was to help users quickly search routes, compare buses, select seats and complete their booking without confusion.

02

The Problem

01

Finding the right bus

Users can find it difficult to compare different buses and timings quickly.

02

Too much information

Important details can get lost inside crowded booking interfaces.

03

Booking friction

Unnecessary steps can make the booking process feel complicated.

03

Research & Insights

I explored common problems users face while searching and booking bus tickets online.

Users want quick route comparison.
Price and timing should be visible.
Seat availability should be easy to understand.
Checkout should require minimum effort.
04 Key Insights
04

User Journey

01

Search

Enter travel details

02

Explore

View available buses

03

Compare

Check price & timing

04

Book

Select seat & pay

05

Wireframes

I created low-fidelity wireframes to define the structure and information hierarchy before moving into the final visual design.

06

Design Decisions

Simple Search

Kept the search experience focused so users can enter their travel details quickly.

Clear Hierarchy

Important information such as price, timing and availability gets stronger visual priority.

Faster Checkout

Reduced unnecessary steps to make the booking flow easier to complete.

07

Final UI

The final interface combines clear information, simple navigation and a focused booking experience.

08

Key Learnings

This project helped me understand how information hierarchy can directly affect a user's ability to make quick decisions.

I also learned how research, wireframing and usability thinking can shape a smoother digital experience.
